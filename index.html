FENCE CALCULATOR GUIDE

How Does a Fence Calculator Work?

A fence calculator estimates the materials required for a fencing project by using the total fence length, post spacing, fence style, and other measurements.

Calculating Fence Posts

The approximate number of fence sections can be calculated by dividing the fence length by the selected post spacing. Posts are then required at the beginning and end of each section.

Calculating Fence Rails

Rails are estimated by multiplying the number of fence sections by the number of rails used in each section.

Calculating Pickets

For picket fences, the required number of pickets depends on the picket width and the gap between adjacent pickets.

Adding Waste

A waste allowance can account for cuts, damaged materials, measurement differences, and other installation losses.

FENCE TYPES

Common Fence Types

Privacy Fence

Privacy fences are commonly designed with closely spaced boards or pickets to provide greater visual privacy.

Picket Fence

Picket fences normally use individual pickets installed with a defined space between them.

Panel Fence

Panel fences use prefabricated sections that are installed between supporting posts.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a fence calculator?

A fence calculator provides an estimate based on the measurements entered. Actual quantities can change because of fence layout, terrain, gates, corners, local requirements, and installation methods.

How far apart should fence posts be?

Post spacing depends on the fence design, materials, height, wind exposure, and installation method. Many fence designs use spacing around 6 to 8 feet, but the appropriate spacing should be confirmed for your specific fence system and local requirements.

Does the calculator include waste?

Yes. You can enter a waste percentage and the calculator will include that allowance in the estimated material quantities.

Can I use the calculator for different fence types?

Yes. The calculator supports privacy, picket, and panel fence calculations. Different fence designs can require different materials and installation methods.
